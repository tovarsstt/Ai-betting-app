# MLB NICHE VARIABLES + HEURISTICS

## ATS / RUN LINE HISTORICAL TRENDS
- **HOME FAVORITES -1.5 (Run Line)**: Cover at ~58% when the starter has ERA <3.50 and K/9 >9.0.
- **ROAD UNDERDOGS +1.5**: Cover at ~61% — the safest run line play in all sports.
- **ROAD UNDERDOGS ML (+120 to +180)**: Win at ~42% but are profitable due to the plus-money juice. Long-term ROI positive.
- **HEAVY FAVORITES (>-200 ML)**: Win ~68% but ROI is negative due to juice. NEVER bet heavy favorites on ML without F5 confirmation.

## PITCHER CONTEXT (use REAL PITCHER STATS block if present)
- ERA/K9/WHIP: Cite exact numbers. ERA <3.50 = elite. WHIP <1.10 = elite. K9 >10 = high strikeout value.
- PITCHER REST: 5+ days rest → ace performs better. 3 days rest → expect shorter outing, higher ERA, fade K props.
- HANDEDNESS PLATOON: LHP vs R-heavy lineup → lean pitcher. RHP vs L-heavy lineup → lean pitcher.
- **3RD TIME THROUGH ORDER**: Most pitchers see a **+0.40 ERA bump** the 3rd time through. If starter has a high 3rd-time ERA, bet Over on hits in the 5th/6th inning.

## UMPIRE IMPACT (cite the assigned home plate umpire)
- **TIGHT ZONE UMPS** (e.g., Pat Hoberg, Nic Lentz): K rate +12% above average. Lean **OVER on K props**, Under on walks.
- **WIDE ZONE UMPS** (e.g., Angel Hernandez, CB Bucknor): Walk rate +15% above average. Lean **OVER on walks**, Under on Ks.
- **RUN IMPACT**: Tight-zone umps produce ~0.4 fewer runs/game. Wide-zone → ~0.3 more runs/game.
- **ALWAYS CHECK**: The daily umpire assignment. It is the single most overlooked variable in MLB betting.

## PARK FACTORS (memorize these)
- COORS FIELD (COL) = +20–25% runs above average. Always lean Over and fade pitchers here.
- PETCO PARK (SD), ORACLE PARK (SF), KAUFFMAN (KC) = -15–20% runs. Strong lean Under, strong pitcher ERA support.
- YANKEE STADIUM (NYY), FENWAY (BOS), GREAT AMERICAN (CIN) = +10–15% runs. Lean Over, especially on windy days.
- All others: neutral ±5%. Park factor context = cite the specific park name in rationale.

## WEATHER & AIR DENSITY
- **AIR DENSITY (The Coors Effect Everywhere)**: 
  - High Temp + High Humidity = **Low Air Density**. Balls travel 3-5% further. Lean **OVER** on team totals and HR props.
  - Cold + Dry = **Heavy Air**. Fade flyball hitters.
- If WEATHER block is absent → game is in a DOMED/RETRACTABLE-ROOF stadium. Controlled environment = neutral.
- Wind blowing OUT 15+mph + temp >80°F = lean Over (1–1.5 run adjustment).
- Wind blowing IN 15+mph + temp <60°F = lean Under (1–1.5 run adjustment).
- Rain delay risk → lean Under (pitchers shaken, relievers used early).

## BULLPEN ATTRITION
- If a team used their 3 best relievers in back-to-back games, their **Win Probability drops by ~4%** in the 7th-9th innings.
- Team's RP thrown 12+ IP in last 3 days → vulnerable late game. Lean Over 7th-9th inning total.
- **BULLPEN ERA SPLIT**: If a team's bullpen ERA is >4.50 vs <3.00 opponent → lean Over on full game total.

## REGRESSION SIGNALS
- BABIP >.320 sustained = luck factor. Fade that pitcher next 2 weeks (regression incoming).
- Pythagorean W%: RS^1.83/(RS^1.83+RA^1.83). If actual W% > Pythagorean by >8% → team is overperforming. Fade them.

## VARIANCE
- **Standard Deviation (Final Margin)**: MLB σ = **3.0 runs**. Much tighter than NBA/NFL.
- A 1-run game is essentially a coinflip. Only bet ML on games where the edge is clear (ace + park + weather aligned).

## SHARP BETS
- F5 (First 5 Innings): Best when ace vs weak offense. Removes bullpen variance entirely.
- NRFI (No Run First Inning): Both aces starting in pitcher park = NRFI best value. Sharp single only, never a parlay leg.

## SGP RULES
- Pitcher strikeouts Over + Under hits allowed + Pitcher to win = clean triple correlation on ace days.
- AVOID run lines in parlays — low payout vs blowout bust risk.
