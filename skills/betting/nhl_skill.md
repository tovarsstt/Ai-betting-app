# NHL NICHE VARIABLES + HEURISTICS — CTE LOCKS v1.0

## CURRENT CONTEXT (June 2026)
NHL Stanley Cup Playoffs are the primary market (April–June). Regular season ends April, playoffs run through June.
Playoff hockey = completely different animal than regular season. Goaltending variance compresses, defense tightens, scoring drops.
Stanley Cup Futures: early rounds are mispriced — market overweights Presidents' Trophy winner by 8–12%.

## PUCK LINE (±1.5) HISTORICAL TRENDS
- **ROAD UNDERDOGS +1.5**: Cover at ~59% overall. Best value in NHL betting — the single most reliable puck line edge.
- **HOME FAVORITES -1.5**: Cover at ~41%. FADE hard. Home favorite wins by 2+ less than half the time.
- **PLAYOFF PUCK LINE**: Home favorite -1.5 covers only ~38% in playoffs. Defense-first hockey = 1-goal games common.
- **FAVORITES -200+ ML**: Implied ~67% but actual win rate ~62%. Heavy chalk = negative ROI. Always take +1.5 instead.
- **DOGS +180 to +250 ML**: Win ~30% but profitable long-term due to plus-money. NHL has highest upset rate in pro sports (28%).

## GOALTENDING IS THE SINGLE BIGGEST VARIABLE
- **SV% <.900**: Vulnerable goalie. Lean Over total and opponent's team total. Flag immediately.
- **SV% .900–.915**: Average. Neutral.
- **SV% .920+**: Elite. Lean Under team total for opposing team. Lean Under full game total.
- **GSAx (Goals Saved Above Expected)**: Best goalie metric. Positive GSAx = goalie stealing wins. Negative = getting exposed.
- **Starter Confirmation**: Never bet NHL without confirmed starter. Backup in = instant line swing of 0.5–1.0 puck line.
- **Back-to-Back Goalie Fatigue**: Starting same goalie B2B = 2nd game SV% drops ~0.008. Lean Over.
- **Playoff Goalie Lockout**: Playoff coaches ride their starter. B2B less of a factor than regular season. But 3-games-in-4-nights is still significant.

## SPECIAL TEAMS (PP% and PK%)
- **PP% >25%**: Elite power play. Lean Over team total when facing penalty-prone opponent.
- **PP% <15%**: Weak power play. Neutral factor.
- **PK% >85%**: Elite penalty kill. Lean Under opposing team total vs this team.
- **PK% <78%**: Sieve PK. Lean Over opposing team total and BTTS (both teams to score).
- **Penalty Rate**: Refs in playoff hockey call fewer penalties. Power plays drop ~20% vs regular season. Don't over-rely on PP advantage.
- **4-on-4 and 3-on-3 (OT)**: High-danger chance creation. BTTS lean in OT situations.

## GAME FLOW + PACE METRICS
- **Corsi For % (CF%)**: Team shot attempt %. CF% >55% = possession-dominant. Lean their puck line and team total.
- **Expected Goals For % (xGF%)**: Better than Corsi. xGF% >55% = structural edge for that team. xGF% <45% = fade.
- **High-Danger Scoring Chances**: Most predictive of actual goals. HDSC differential >5/game = significant edge.
- **PDO (SV% + Sh%)**: PDO >102 = team getting lucky (regression coming). PDO <98 = unlucky (positive regression ahead).
  PDO is the single best regression-to-mean signal in hockey.
- **Zone Start %**: High offensive zone starts (>55%) = inflated Corsi. Adjust down. High defensive zone starts (<45%) = suppressed Corsi. Adjust up.

## SCHEDULE + REST FACTORS
- **3-in-4 Nights**: Team on third game in four nights → fatigue is real. Fade their ML and team total. Lean opponent.
- **5+ Days Rest**: Over-rested = rusty. Neutral to slight fade in regular season. In playoffs = teams value rest highly.
- **Travel**: Cross-country travel (Pacific to Eastern time zone) + day-game = fade road team ML.
- **Divisional Familiarity**: Division rivals know each other well. Totals lean Under in divisional matchups (familiarity = tighter defense).
- **Playoff Series Momentum**: After winning game 1 → series winner ~65%. After winning game 2 in a 0-0 series → series winner ~70%.
- **Elimination Game (Facing 3-0 Hole)**: Never bet the team facing elimination in game 4 of a sweep. Win rate ~20%.

## TOTAL (OVER/UNDER) NICHE TRENDS
- **Playoff Totals**: Books set them too high early in series. Under hits ~56% in playoff games 1-2 before adjusting.
- **Game 7**: Most bet game in any series. Books shade sharp. Fade the public side. Under hits ~59% in Game 7s historically.
- **After High-Scoring Game (7+ goals)**: Next game Under hits ~61%. Goalies adjust, coaches tighten defense.
- **Both Teams Score (BTTS)**: Better than total in low-scoring games. BTTS Yes hits ~67% in games with totals set at 5.5+.
- **1st Period Goals**: Best derivative market. First period avg ~1.3 goals. Over 0.5 1P goals hits ~72%.
- **60-Minute Line vs Full-Game**: Full game includes OT/SO — more variance. If backing underdog, bet full-game ML (OT/SO coin flip).

## ARENA + HOME ICE FACTORS
- **Canadian Arenas (Montreal, Toronto, Ottawa)**: Crowd noise = real factor. Home ML boost ~2-3%. Playoff atmosphere amplified.
- **Altitude**: None — all arenas at sea level or near it. Not a factor in NHL.
- **Ice Surface Quality**: Late-season playoff ice can be soft (building events). Slower ice = slower pace = Under lean.
- **Playoff Home Ice**: Home team wins ~55% in playoffs (lower than regular season 59%). Home advantage compressed but real.

## SERIES BETTING (FUTURES)
- **Series Price vs Implied ML**: If a team is -150 in each game but listed at -130 to win series, there's math arbitrage.
- **Series Length**: Over 5.5 games hits ~55% historically. Series rarely end in sweeps (only ~20% of series).
- **Goalie Matchup**: Series winner = team with better goalie 70% of the time. More important than any other factor.
- **Coaching Matchup**: Track record in adjustments game-to-game. Teams that adjust series-to-series win more often.

## KEY NUMBERS IN NHL
- 1 goal games: ~35% of all NHL games. Puck line +1.5 is massive value on dogs.
- OT/SO: ~24% of regular season games go to OT. Leans full-game underdog ML slightly.
- Empty net goals: ~80% of games with lead in final 2 min. Affects full-game total but not 60-min line.

## BETTING STRUCTURE RULES
- **Best single bet**: Puck line road dog +1.5 when goalie matchup favors underdog.
- **Best parlay leg**: Road underdog +1.5 + Under — correlation is positive (close low-scoring games).
- **Avoid**: Home favorite -1.5 as parlay anchor. Too low a cover rate. Kills parlays.
- **SGP**: Team win ML + Under team total opponent + star player Anytime Goal scorer = clean correlation on defensive team.

## SHARP SIGNAL INTEGRATION
- Pinnacle sharp line: NHL lines move fast after starter announcement. Gap ≥0.5 puck line units = sharp action.
- Late line movement toward dog = sharp side. Follow it.
- B2B + starter unconfirmed = wait. The line moves 0.5–1.0 after confirmation.
