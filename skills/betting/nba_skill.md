## FINALS / PLAYOFF SERIES CONTEXT
- Game 1: Favorite covers ~51%. Home team wins 65% of Game 1s. Lean home, not blindly.
- After road win Game 1: Road team covers ATS at 54% in Game 2. Home team emotionally depleted.
- After home team loses Game 1 at home: They cover 57% in Game 2 (desperate).
- Series elimination games (facing elimination): Home team covers 58%. Road team covers 51%.
- Game 5 of a tied series: Highest variance game. Total often goes Over (both teams attacking).
- **Blowout risk in Finals is LOWER** — teams are more evenly matched. Fade -10+ spreads more than regular season.
- Carry-over injury context: Stars playing limited minutes (foot/knee niggles) = prop Unders.

## ADVANCED NICHE STATS — ALWAYS CITE
- **Net Rating (NetRtg)**: Points per 100 possessions differential. Best team quality metric.
  NetRtg drop >8 pts without star → real line value. Cite the number.
- **OffRtg / DefRtg**: Points per 100 possessions each end. Mismatch >6 pts = structural edge.
- **Pace (Possessions/48)**: Fast (>102) vs slow (<96) → slow team controls tempo ~60%. Fade fast team's total.
- **True Shooting % (TS%)**: TS% >60% = elite offense.
- **Usage Rate**: Star out → 2nd/3rd options spike 5-8%. Their Overs become +EV.
- **Box Plus/Minus (BPM)**: BPM >+5 = superstar tier.
- **On/Off Net Rating**: Team +12 on / -4 off for star = enormous. Fade total if star questionable.
- **5-Man Lineup Net Rating**: Closing lineup tells you who wins close games.
- **3PT Rate Allowed**: Bottom-10 opponent 3PA allowed + high-volume 3P team = lean Over on 3PM props.
- **Turnover Rate vs Steal Rate**: High-TO offense vs high-steal defense = possession edge worth ~2 pts.

## ATS HISTORICAL TRENDS
- Home favorites -1 to -5: ~52.5%. Slight edge.
- Home favorites -6 to -10: ~49.8%. No edge.
- Home favorites -10+: ~46.3%. **FADE TERRITORY**. Garbage time backdoor covers.
- Road underdogs +3 to +7: ~53.2%. Sweet spot.
- Playoff dogs: ~52.8%. Series familiarity narrows margins.

## REFEREE IMPACT
- **High foul refs** (Scott Foster, Tony Brothers): 44+ fouls/game. Lean Over total. Star FT prop Overs.
- **Low foul refs** (Ed Malloy): 38 fouls/game. Lean Under. Stars get fewer calls.
- Check referee assignment before every game. It's a real edge.

## FATIGUE & SCHEDULE
- **B2B**: Team on B2B loses ~3.1 pts vs rested. Both on B2B = neutral.
- **3-in-4 nights**: ~4.2 pts loss. Stars sit or play limited minutes. Fade starter props.
- **Travel attrition**: 3+ time zones road trip = ~1.5 pts fade.
- **Rest advantage ≥2 days**: Covers 55.3% ATS.
- In Finals: No B2B. But short turnaround after long travel series matters.

## PLAYER PROPS — ALPHA ZONE
- **Usage Redistribution**: Star sits/questionable → 2nd/3rd options spike 5-8%. Book slow to adjust. Morning window.
- **Minutes Threshold**: 34+ min avg = reliable prop. <28 min = too much variance, fade.
- **Blowout Risk**: Spread -12+ → fade ALL starter props on the fav. Garbage-time DNPs.
- **Assist Props**: Spike when team spacing improves (more 3PA = kick-out opportunities).
- **Rebounds**: Big man vs top-5 OREB opponent = Over. Safe SGP add (weakly correlated with game outcome).
- **Steals/Blocks**: Only bet when player top-15 in category AND opponent top-10 in opposite (high TOs or FGA rate).
- **Foul trouble props**: High-foul-rate ref + star in foul trouble in first half = fade their 2nd half Minutes Over.

## PACE & VARIANCE
- **NBA σ = 11.5 points**. Use this for alt-line probability math.
- High pace (both top-10 Pace): Lean Over.
- Low pace (both bottom-10 Pace): Lean Under.
- Pace mismatch: Slow team controls tempo ~60%. Fade fast team's total.

---

## SGP CORRELATION RULES
- **POSITIVE**: Team Win + Star Player Over Points (+0.75). Team Over Total + Player Over Assists (+0.65). Team Win + Rebounds Over for dominant big (physical style).
- **NEGATIVE**: Team Win + Opponent Star Over Points (unless spread ≥12). Team Total Under + Player Points Over on the fav.
- **SAFE ADD**: Player Rebounds (weakly correlated). Q1 Total Over (fast-pace game signal).
- **1H SGP**: 1H spread + 1H total is sharpest NBA market. Less garbage time noise.

## SHARP SIGNALS
- CLV (Closing Line Value): Line closes 2+ pts worse than your bet = you had sharp value.
- Steam move: Line moves 2+ pts in <30 min across 3+ books = follow it.
- RLM: 70%+ public on one side, line moves opposite = sharp money on other side.
- Pinnacle gap ≥2 pts over DK/FD: exploit the soft book immediately.
