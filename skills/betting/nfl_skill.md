## ADVANCED NICHE STATS — ALWAYS CITE
- **DVOA (Defense-adjusted Value Over Average)**: Team efficiency metric adjusted for opponent strength. DVOA gap >10% = structural edge worth ~2 pts.
- **EPA/Play (Expected Points Added per play)**: Offensive EPA/play differential >0.15 = lean toward that offense.
- **CPOE (Completion % Over Expected)**: Measures QB accuracy vs expected given target depth/context. CPOE >+5% = elite-tier QB. Negative CPOE = regression candidate.
- **Air Yards per attempt**: Deep ball attack vs soft zone = Over on passing yards prop.
- **Pressure Rate**: QB under pressure >35% of dropbacks = fade that QB's passing yards/TD props.
- **Line Yards (rushing metric)**: Measures O-line run blocking, not just RB talent. Top-10 line yards team vs bottom-10 gap-shooting defense = RB rush yards Over.
- **Yards After Contact per attempt**: Measures RB individual quality. >2.5 YAC = elite broken-tackle runner.
- **Defensive Snap Share**: A key edge rusher missing (90%+ snap share) = +15% sack rate reduction for opponent QB.
- **Red Zone TD% vs RZ Scoring%**: Team converting TDs not FGs in red zone = points-per-drive edge. Lean game total Over.
- **3rd Down Conversion Rate**: 50%+ vs 35%- defense = sustained possession → lean team total Over.
- **O-Line Pass Block Win Rate**: Top-10 O-line vs top-10 D-line pass rush = QB prop protection.

## ATS HISTORICAL TRENDS
- **HOME FAVORITES -1 to -3**: Cover at ~51.8%. Coin flip territory.
- **HOME FAVORITES -3.5 to -7**: Cover at ~49.2%. Key numbers make this dangerous.
- **HOME FAVORITES -7.5 to -10**: Cover at ~47.5%. FADE — books know public loves big home favorites.
- **HOME FAVORITES -10+**: Cover at ~44.1%. **HARD FADE**. NFL parity is real.
- **ROAD UNDERDOGS +3 to +7**: Cover at ~53.5%. The NFL ATS sweet spot.
- **ROAD UNDERDOGS +7+**: Cover at ~52.1%. Still value but lower confidence.
- **PLAYOFF DOGS**: ATS 54.2% historically. Parity intensifies in the postseason.

## KEY NUMBERS (non-negotiable)
- 3 and 7 are sacred. Half-point off 3 or 7 worth 3–5% win probability.
- Key sequence: 3, 6, 7, 10, 13, 14, 17. Never buy past 10 unless price is -105 or better.
- Line moves from -2.5 to -3.5 = enormous (FG margin). From -4 to -5 = negligible.

## REFEREE CREW IMPACT
- **FLAG-HAPPY CREWS**: Some crews average 14+ penalties/game (e.g., Brad Allen). This extends drives → lean **OVER** on game total.
- **TIGHT CREWS**: Crews averaging 9 penalties/game → lean **UNDER** on penalty-related yardage and game pace.
- **PASS INTERFERENCE**: Crews with high DPI call rates → boost WR deep-threat props.

## WEATHER FORMULA (use WEATHER DATA block)
- Wind >15mph → fade all passing props -30%, lean Under on game total.
- Wind >25mph → hard Under, fade QBs entirely, lean Run game props.
- Rain + cold + wind = compound effect. Each adds ~0.5 pts to Under confidence.
- Dome teams (Chiefs, Saints, Rams, Vikings) playing outdoors in cold/wind = -2 pt adjustment.
- **ALTITUDE**: Denver (5,280 ft) — visiting teams gas out in 4th quarter. Broncos cover at 56% ATS at home.

## REST + TRAVEL (The Jet Lag Edge)
- **TRAVEL & TIME ZONES**: West Coast teams traveling to the East Coast for a **1 PM ET kickoff** have a historical ATS disadvantage. Fade West teams in 1st Half.
- Bye week team vs no-bye = +3.2 pts historical edge (biggest rest edge in sports).
- Short week (Thursday game) = -1.7 pts. Books rarely fully adjust.
- West Coast team playing 10am local time = -1.5 pts. Circadian disruption.
- Long travel (LAX → NYC in winter) = -0.8 pts on road side.

## SITUATION EDGES
- **DIVISIONAL DOGS**: ATS ~54% historically. Familiarity flattens lines. If +7 or more = high-conviction value.
- **TURNOVER REGRESSION**: Team +5 in turnover margin over last 3 games → regression coming. Fade them.
- **REVENGE SPOT**: Team coming off embarrassing loss (>17 pts) at home = +2.5 pts ATS edge.
- **LETDOWN SPOT**: After huge emotional win (divisional/primetime), -1.5 ATS letdown the following week.
- **PRIMETIME ROAD UNDERDOG**: +7 or more in primetime = +56% ATS (national broadcast = motivation/eye test boost).
- **DOME TEAM OUTDOORS IN COLD**: Chiefs, Saints, Rams, Vikings outside in <35°F = -2 pt adjustment.

## VARIANCE
- **NFL σ = 13.5 points**. Any game within 1 TD (7 pts) = essentially a coin flip.
- Key number awareness: 3-pt games occur 15.5% of time. 7-pt games occur 9.4%. These are the sacred margins.

## PLAYER PROPS NICHE
- **QB Passing Yards**: Air yards per attempt >9.0 AND clean pocket time >2.8s = lean Over. Wind >15mph = mandatory Under.
- **WR Receiving Yards**: Target share >25% AND cushion coverage (CB playing 5+ yards off) = lean Over. Slot WR in zone-heavy defense = lean Over.
- **RB Rushing Yards**: Team top-10 run DVOA + opponent bottom-10 = lean Over. O-line run block win rate >65% = confirmation signal.
- **TE Receiving**: Red zone TE usage >3 targets/game + team in top-10 red zone frequency = lean Over TDs.
- **Kicker Props**: Distance 45-54 yd FG make % for each kicker varies wildly. Windy outdoor game = fade long FG props.

## SGP RULES
- **CLEAN TRIPLE**: QB Over passing yards + WR1 Over receiving yards + Team Win = strong correlation in dome/no-wind pass-heavy games.
- **NEVER PAIR**: QB passing yards + RB rushing yards (run-pass balance kills both). TD scorer props in SGP (too volatile, single play = bust).
- **SAFE SGP ADD**: Anytime Passer TD + Team Win (strong +0.8 correlation). Team Total Over + QB Over passing yards (same drive, same correlation).
- **1H SGP**: 1H spread + 1H total is the sharpest market in NFL. Smaller sample = less garbage time noise.
